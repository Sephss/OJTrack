# 🧭 OJT Companion

**Your internship journey, organized.**

An Android app for students doing their On-the-Job Training (OJT) or internship. Log your daily activities, jot quick notes, and track your rendered hours toward your required total.

## Features

- **Home:** OJT progress, quick actions, and a weekly activity graph
- **Journal:** daily activity log with categories and search
- **Notes:** quick notes with pinning and color-coded categories
- **Hours:** Time In / Time Out, breaks, and attendance history
